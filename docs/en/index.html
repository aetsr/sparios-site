Sparios

A calm spending journal that keeps your data on your device.

Your bank’s app only shows its own card. Sparios records your Apple Pay spending whichever card you pay with, right next to your rent, bills and anything you add by hand. It all stays on your iPhone. No account, no server, no ads.
