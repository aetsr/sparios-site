Every payment, already written down

Set up one Shortcuts automation and every Apple Pay payment lands in Sparios a moment later, with the shop and the amount. Rent, bills and cash go in the same list.

Know what’s left

Add your income once. Rent, subscriptions and loan payments are booked on their day, and Sparios shows what’s left this month and how much you can spend per day.

See the month-end coming

Compass reads your habits and shows where this month is heading. Every evening Sparios draws its own line. Spend less than that line and you win the month.

Ask about your money

“How much did I spend on groceries this month?” The Assistant answers from your own entries, right on your iPhone.

A tiny office runs it all

Five pixel colleagues live in Sparios. The courier brings in today’s receipts, the sorter files them, the navigator works out your month-end. Tap one to see what they’re up to.
