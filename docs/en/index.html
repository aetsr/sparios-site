A look inside

Everything your month needs. Nothing it doesn’t.

Every payment, already written down

Set up one Shortcuts automation and every Apple Pay payment lands in Sparios a moment later. Rent, bills and cash go in the same list.

Know what’s left

Add your income once. Rent and subscriptions are booked on their day, and you always see what’s left and your daily share.

See the month-end coming

Compass shows where this month is heading. Each evening Sparios draws its own line. Stay under it and you win the month.

Ask about your money

“How much did I spend on groceries this month?” The Assistant answers from your own entries, on your iPhone.

A tiny office

Five pixel colleagues bring in your receipts, file them and work out your month-end. Tap one to see what they’re up to.

Quiet by design

At most one notification a day, in the evening. Nothing at night. Pick a category right from the notification.
