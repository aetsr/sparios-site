Accessibility

What we do so everyone can use Sparios comfortably.

Last updated: 18 September 2026

Our goal

We want everyone — including people with visual, hearing, motor or cognitive disabilities — to use Sparios comfortably.

What’s supported

VoiceOver: Buttons, charts and cards carry meaningful labels; chart values are also available as a readable list.

Dynamic Type: Text scales up to the largest accessibility sizes and wraps instead of being cut off.

Dark Mode and contrast: Text colors are chosen for legible contrast in light and dark; increases and decreases are shown with arrows and words, not color alone.

Reduce Motion: Animations are simplified when this setting is on.

Touch targets: Interactive elements are at least 44 × 44 pt.

Ran into a barrier?

Email tasarahmeteren@gmail.com. Tell us which assistive technology and screen you were using and we’ll prioritise a fix.
